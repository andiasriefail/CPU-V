# Privacy Policy – CPU-V

Last updated: 2026

## Summary

CPU-V reads information about your own device and shows it on your screen. It does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share your data.

## Data the app reads

Hardware and system details (including the Android device ID, shown on screen only and never sent anywhere), battery, display, memory and storage, sensors, and network details such as IP address, Wi-Fi name and mobile network type (read without special permissions). This data is read locally and shown only inside the app.

The Microphone test measures the live sound level on your device. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## Data the app stores

- App settings (language and theme) and test results (passed or failed) are stored locally on your device.
- One chipset logo (SVG) is downloaded from the configured URL and kept in the app's private storage.

No account is needed. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Network use

The only network request made by the app is the one-time download of the chipset logo. The request is a normal HTTPS request, so the image host may see your IP address.

## Permissions

- Location: only so Android allows reading the Wi-Fi name. Your location is never read, stored or sent.

- Nearby devices (Bluetooth): only to show the adapter state, its capabilities and the names of paired devices on your screen. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Microphone: only for the Microphone test, to show a live level meter.

You can deny or revoke permissions at any time in system settings. The app continues to work with the remaining information.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
